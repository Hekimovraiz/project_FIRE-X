# NASA PSI Microgravity Combustion Datasets (2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge — "Flame in Freefall")

This directory contains the empirical microgravity combustion datasets sourced from the **NASA Physical Science Informatics (PSI)** repository (`psi.nasa.gov`) strictly matching the investigations specified in the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge: "Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data"**.

## Active Challenge Datasets (569 Canonical Experiments across 18 Investigation Sub-Suites / 6 Core Programs)

1. **`PSI-69_Experimental table_FLEX.csv`** (`274` tests) — **Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX)** in the ISS Combustion Integrated Rack (**CIR**) with the Multi-user Droplet Combustion Apparatus (**MDCA**): spherical droplet combustion (`n-Heptane`, `Methanol`), cool-flame phenomena, and inert gas suppression with $\text{CO}_2$ (up to 70%) and $\text{He}$ (up to 45%).
2. **`FLEX2_Cool_Flame_Droplet_Data.csv`** (`10` tests) — **FLEX-2 & Cool Flames Investigation (CFI)** in ISS CIR (MDCA): two-stage droplet combustion and cool-flame transition limits.
3. **`BASS1_Initial_ISS_Data.csv`** (`8` tests) & **`PSI-25_Experimental table_BASS-II.csv`** (`129` tests) — **Burning and Suppression of Solids (BASS & BASS-II)** in the ISS Microgravity Science Glovebox (**MSG**) using the modular fan-driven flow duct built for SPICE: solid materials (fabrics, PMMA rods/slabs/spheres) under forced airflow ($0\text{–}55\text{ cm/s}$).
4. **`PSI-98_Experimental table_SAFFIRE-1.csv`** (`4` burns) & **`SAFFIRE_II_to_VI_Flight_Data.csv`** (`7` burns across **SAFFIRE-II** and **SAFFIRE-III**) — **Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration (Saffire-I, -II, and -III)** aboard autonomous **Cygnus** cargo spacecraft after ISS departure.
5. **`ACME_CIR_Extinction_Data.csv`** (`8` tests) & **`ACME_Extended_Research_Data.csv`** (`60` tests) — **Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments (ACME)** in ISS CIR modular hardware: **BRE** (Burning Rate Emulator simulating solid material flammability), **CFI-G / CFI** (Cool Flames Investigation with Gases), **E-FIELD Flames** (electric-field partial-gravity mimicry), **CLD** (Coflow Laminar Diffusion Flame), **Flame Design**, and **s-Flame**.
6. **`SLICE_Extinguishment_Data.csv`** (`8` tests) — **Structure and Liftoff In Combustion Experiment (SLICE)** in the ISS MSG fan-driven flow duct.
7. **`NASA_SAME_Aerosol_Detector_Data.csv`** (`50` tests) — **Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment (SAME)** in ISS MSG: smoke particle size distribution, coagulation/aging, and ionization vs. photoelectric detector response.
8. **`NASA_PSI_Complete_Suite_CFIG_SPICE_DAFT.csv`** (`11` tests) — **ACME CFI-G** (`4` tests up to $85\%\ \text{O}_2$ inside-out flames), **SPICE** (`3` tests: Smoke Point In Co-flow Experiment in ISS MSG), **SAME-R** (`2` tests: Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment-Reflight), and **DAFT / DAFT-2** (`2` tests: Dust and Aerosol Measurement Feasibility Test in ISS MSG).

> **Note on Partial-Gravity Data (LUCI & FM2):** Per the official NASA Space Apps Challenge prompt, *"there is currently no partial-gravity combustion data available in PSI. We expect such data will be added in the future, for example from the Lunar-g Combustion Investigation (LUCI) and the Flammability of Materials on the Moon (FM2) experiment. Your tool can still be used to bound the expected behavior in partial gravity by pairing results from microgravity and normal gravity."*
